Sparse DETRVision
ICLR 2022

Sparse DETR: Efficient End-to-End Object Detection with Learnable Sparsity
Wuhyun Shin*, Byungseok Roh*, JaeWoong Shin*, Saehoon Kim (* equal contribution)

Paper (arXiv)ICLR poster & talkCode (GitHub)

Goal
Cut the memory and compute cost of Deformable DETR further while improving accuracy.
Prior work
Key sparsification reduces attention complexity, but every query stays dense.
Approach
  • Learnable query sparsification: learn which encoder tokens the decoder will actually reference, and update only those (memory and speed).
  • An auxiliary loss on encoder blocks to prevent vanishing gradients (accuracy).
Result
Beat Deformable DETR on COCO with only 10% of encoder tokens, cutting total computation by 38% and raising FPS by 42%.
Role
Ideas, discussion, experiments, and writing.
SCRLVision
CVPR 2021

Spatially Consistent Representation Learning
Wuhyun Shin*, Byungseok Roh*, Ildoo Kim, Sungwoong Kim (* equal contribution)

Paper (arXiv)Code (GitHub)

Goal
Pre-train backbone weights suited to localization tasks such as detection and segmentation.
Prior work
Contrastive learning builds two augmented views from different crops, so it easily misses the spatial consistency of local representations.
Approach
Pick the same image region (the same semantics) in both augmented views, and train their representations to match.
Result
Outperformed global-matching methods such as BYOL and SwAV on localization tasks: +4.0 AP over ImageNet-supervised pre-training and +2.2 AP over BYOL on PASCAL VOC detection.
Role
The whole process from idea to submission; refactored and released the code.

Meta-learned SamplerAutoML
2019

KAIST School of Computing

Slides (PDF)Code (GitHub)

Goal
A minibatch selection model that picks training data to fit the learning progress — a meta-learning problem that learns the data sampling distribution itself with a neural network.
Approach
  • CONCRETE masks trained with second-order gradients: mask loss terms to select samples.
  • Sampling with reinforcement learning: the model's training outcome is the reward and sampling is the action.
Result
Showed curriculum-like behavior: harder data was sampled more often.
Role
Full-time joint research with a Ph.D. student, aimed at a first-author paper.
Meta-learned OptimizerAutoML
2019

KAIST School of Computing

Slides (PDF)Code (GitHub)Paper draft (PDF)

Goal
Minimize the generalization gap and wall-clock cost of learned optimizers — a meta-learning problem that learns the parameter update rule itself with a neural network.
Approach
  • Uncertainty-aware loss-surface estimation and observation, to cut the number of back-propagations.
  • Selective coordinate updates: induce sparsity and back-propagate and update only some parameters.
Result
Beat static-rule optimizers such as SGD and Adam on some tasks.
Role
Full-time solo research; drafted a first-author paper.
