Applied AI/ML Engineer · 6+ years in industry · 5+ years of graduate research

I'm an ML engineer at TMAP Mobility, where I build recommendation models and a real-time pricing model for a designated-driver service, along with the MLOps pipelines that ship them to production.

Previously, I was an AI researcher at Kakao Brain, where I co-first-authored papers at ICLR 2022 and CVPR 2021. Before that, I spent three years in Prof. Sung Ju Hwang's MLAI Lab, which moved from UNIST to KAIST, first as research staff and then as a Ph.D. student. Earlier, I built robots and vision systems for my M.S. in the Robotics & Vision Lab at Sungkyunkwan University.
